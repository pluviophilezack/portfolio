ABOUT ME

I am Zack You, a professional curriculum designer dedicated to assisting educators in optimizing both in-person and online courses to enhance learning experiences and teaching effectiveness. Additionally, I serve as a high school lecturer, specializing in using guided discussions to inspire students' critical thinking and cultivate their ability for independent learning.

Currently, I am pursuing a degree in Law and at National Cheng Kung University, equipping myself with a strong academic background in law and social sciences. I have a keen interest in interdisciplinary knowledge and actively engage in various recreational and sports activities, such as darts, board games, badminton, and sprinting. Moreover, I am skilled in playing the ukulele, electric guitar, and jazz drums.
